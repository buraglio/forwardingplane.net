Andrew Palardy joins the hosts to discuss how Tayga, the open-source NAT64 daemon for Linux, is being updated to ensure it is up to date with all the current RFCs. The discussion covers RFC compliance updates and UDP checksum fixes for resolving connectivity problems, plus new eBPF CLAT support arriving in NetworkManager to provide automatic CLAT functionality.
