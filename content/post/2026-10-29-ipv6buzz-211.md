Ed, Nick, and Tom are joined by [Jim Alumbaugh](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jimalumbaugh/), Technical Program Manager for Windows Networking Protocols, to discuss the newly released IPv6 Windows CLAT support for Wi-Fi and Ethernet interfaces. Jim explains how this feature enables enterprises to accelerate migration to IPv6-mostly networks. They also examine technical considerations, industry adoption trends, and upcoming PowerShell tools to help track CLAT usage.
